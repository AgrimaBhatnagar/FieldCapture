# Evaluation report (offline pipeline, no API)

Generated by `python eval/make_report.py` from `eval/results.json` (produced by `python eval/run_all.py <images-dir>`).

## Read this first: what these numbers are and are not
- **51 images, uploaded by the user.** 40 are in four folders named for the task (`pic1..pic10`, 736 px wide, Pinterest-style), several look AI-generated (one carries a 'Meta AI' watermark). 11 are WhatsApp photos that look like the user's own. **None were captured with glasses.**
- The brief requires the final eval frames to be the candidate's own captures. **These are development numbers. Re-run on your own 40+ frames before submitting.**
- **Ground truth was read by one labeller (an AI assistant) from the images, with no second reviewer.** It may contain errors, and O/0 style ambiguities were resolved by eye.
- Only 10 nameplates, so every nameplate percentage has a wide error bar (one plate = 10 points; serial n=6, date n=4). Thresholds and parser rules were tuned on the same images, so they are **in-sample**. The damage classifier uses leave-one-out cross-validation, which is out-of-sample.
- Brief minimums (40 frames, 10+ nameplates, 10+ low-light): 51 frames, 10 nameplates, 10 low-light folder images. The low-light folder contains several images that are not actually dark (see below).

Latency per plate on this CPU: **full 16.9 s**, **fast 6.2 s** (fast = 2 of 6 preprocessing variants x 2 page modes). Cost per job: $0 in API fees. Fast mode is ~2.7x faster; it changes accuracy by field (see table), so it is not a free win.

Exact fields: full 24/30, fast 22/30. **Not above 98%: full mode is 80% of fields exact on these 10 plates.** Remaining errors are misreads Tesseract makes consistently (0 vs 9, O vs 0, M01 as N@1, small print), which parsing rules cannot fix without hardcoding answers. A stronger OCR engine (e.g. PaddleOCR, EasyOCR) is the realistic route; not installable offline here, so not tested.

**Negative result (kept for honesty):** an extra pass that re-reads each model/serial crop at high zoom with a restricted character set made accuracy WORSE (full mode model 70% to 40%, serial 67% to 50%; see `eval/ocr_comparison.json`). It is in `ocr.py` but off by default.

Confidence = share of preprocessing variants agreeing x mean Tesseract word confidence. It is lower when wrong for model, serial and date (table), **but not reliably: the 0/9 serial error scored 0.50, higher than the average correct serial (0.44), so a confidently wrong read is not detected**.

**The tree's own confidence is not informative**: mean 0.747 when right vs 0.799 when wrong. The pipeline therefore reports each predicted class's measured leave-one-out precision instead: {'crack': 0.667, 'none': 0.6, 'stain': 0.619, 'standing_water': 0.667}. ## 3. Capture quality gate (v2)

- **Light**: cut at luma 57. Retake-labelled images have luma [27.4, 32.1, 32.4, 33.6, 56.8]; the darkest image I labelled usable is 60.2. Label agreement 100% (in-sample, my own labels, n=51; the separation is a narrow gap 56.8 to 60.2).
- **Blur**: new re-blur ratio (content independent). Real frames fall in [0.41, 0.69]; cut 0.70 gives 0 false rejects. Against **synthetic** blur of the same frames it catches 29% at sigma 1.5 and 90% at sigma 3.0. Mild blur is **mostly missed**: web-compressed images are already soft. **No genuinely blurred real photo exists in this set, so this is unvalidated on real motion blur.**
- **Framing (nameplates only)**: good plates [1.02, 2.38]; cut 1.0 gives 0 false rejects and catches 70% of **synthetic** corner crops.
- **Finding that changed the design**: the original gate (sharpness variance + framing on all photos) rejected 5 of 10 correctly framed wall-crack photos, because a thin crack has little edge energy, and its sharpness cutoff of 40 never triggered (real values 200 to 25,000). Framing now applies to nameplates only; blur uses the ratio.
- Pass rate by folder: {'nameplate': '10/10', 'extra': '11/11', 'home': '10/10', 'lowlight': '5/10', 'wall': '10/10'}. Rejections are all 'too dark' (images ['31', '32', '33', '38', '40']). In the low-light folder 5 of 10 images pass because they are not actually too dark for reading detail.

## 4. What this does not show
- Nothing here was captured on glasses; resolution, motion blur, and auto-exposure on real Meta glasses are untested.
- Voice is a text parser (unit tested), not tested with real speech or the glasses microphone.
- Sample record `eval/sample_job_record.json` is assembled from still images with typed commands. It is not a real walk-through.
